## Medium-specific scope

The viewport sweep applies to screen interfaces. Print, film, and image-generation work are explicitly scoped in [platform guidance](../rules/platform-guidance.md) and retain their existing domain material. Their applicable design, accessibility, identity, color, typography, and composition guidance remains in scope.
